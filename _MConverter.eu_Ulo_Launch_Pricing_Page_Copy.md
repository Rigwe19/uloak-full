**CUSTOMER PROMISE**

**Start free. Pay once for a complete occasion. Subscribe only for an ongoing family archive.**

# 1. Customer-facing opening copy {#customer-facing-opening-copy}

**PRICING**

**Simple pricing for stories that matter**

Start with a free Room. Upgrade once when you need more space and features, or choose a Family Archive to preserve stories together over time.

**Guests never pay to contribute a story.**

## Location selector

**Choose your location**

> **Selector options:** Nigeria \| Rest of Africa \| United Kingdom \| United States / Rest of world \| Europe

*The pricing cards should display only the visitor\'s selected regional price. Do not show five currencies on each customer-facing card.*

| Nigeria | ₦15,000 | ₦3,500 | ₦35,000 | ₦7,000 |

# 2. Starter Room {#starter-room}

**START AT NO COST**

**Free**

A simple way to try Ulo or collect stories from a small group.

## What is included

**Room** One active Starter Room.

**Contributions** Up to 50 guest contributions.

**Storage** 1GB total storage.

**Story formats** Photos, videos, voice notes and written stories.

**Sharing** One private sharing link.

**Guest access** Guests can contribute without paying.

**Collection period** Collect new contributions for 30 days.

**Downloads** Download contributions individually.

**Control** The organiser controls who can access the Room.

> **Create a free Room**

*No card required.*

> **After 30 days:** New contributions close. The organiser can still access and download the stories already collected or upgrade the same Room.

# 3. Full Ulo Room {#full-ulo-room}

**MOST POPULAR**

**One-off payment per Room**

*One occasion. Every voice. One lasting collection.*

> **Use it for:** One wedding, birthday, anniversary, graduation, reunion, burial, funeral, memorial or other meaningful occasion.

## What is included

**Room** One complete Room for one occasion.

**Guests** Unlimited invited guests.

**Contributions** Unlimited contributions within the 10GB storage allowance.

**Story formats** Photos, videos, voice notes and written stories.

**Personalisation** A personalised Room cover and welcome message.

**Invitations** A private contribution link and downloadable QR code.

**Organisation** Review and manage contributions before completing the Room.

**Downloads** Download all collected stories together.

**Keepsake** Create a slideshow from collected memories.

**Online access** 12 months of online Room access.

**Next step** Move the completed Room into an active Family Archive later.

> **Create a Full Room**

*One payment. No monthly subscription. Guests contribute free.*

> **Meaning of per Room:** One birthday is one Room and one payment. A separate wedding, funeral, memorial or future celebration requires a separate Room and payment.

# 4. Family Archive {#family-archive}

**YOUR FAMILY\'S ONGOING STORY**

**Monthly or yearly subscription**

*A private home for family stories, not limited to one occasion.*

## Billing choices

**Monthly** Pay the applicable regional monthly price. The subscription renews monthly until cancelled.

**Yearly** Pay the applicable regional yearly price once each year and save compared with twelve monthly payments.

## What is included

**Archive** One private Family Archive.

**Family access** Invite the whole family with no per-person charge.

**Storage** 25GB total family storage.

**Story formats** Photos, videos, voice notes and written stories.

**Organisation** Organise stories by person, generation, family branch or theme.

**Ongoing capture** Continue receiving stories while the subscription is active.

**Administration** A family administrator controls invitations and access.

**Downloads** Download individual stories or the complete archive.

**Cancellation** Cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

> **Start a Family Archive**

*Choose monthly or yearly billing.*

> **Launch boundary:** The Family Archive does not include Full Event or Tribute Rooms at launch. Those Rooms are purchased separately, but a completed Room can later be moved into an active Family Archive.

# 5. Privacy, payment and renewal copy {#privacy-payment-and-renewal-copy}

**PRIVATE BY DEFAULT**

**Your Ulo Room or Family Archive is private unless you choose to share it.**

## The organiser decides

**Viewing** Who can view the Room or Archive.

**Contributing** Who can submit stories and memories.

**Selection** Which contributions are included.

**Export** When to download the collected stories.

## Payment wording

Prices are based on the customer\'s selected location. Applicable taxes and the final amount will be shown before payment. A bank or card provider may charge a currency-conversion fee when the customer pays in a different currency.

**Full Rooms** One-off purchases. They do not renew automatically.

**Family Archive** Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.

**Cancellation** Family Archive access continues to the end of the billing period already paid for.

## Required policy links

>
> **Important:** Do not state that a displayed price includes or excludes tax until Ulo\'s accountant and checkout configuration confirm the correct treatment for each selling location.

# 6. Frequently asked questions {#frequently-asked-questions}

## **Is the Full Room payment per event or per Room?**

The payment is per Room. In most cases, one Room represents one occasion. A birthday Room requires one payment. A wedding held later requires another Room and another payment. A burial or memorial tribute also has its own Room.

## **Do guests have to pay?**

No. Only the person creating or upgrading the Room pays. Invited guests can contribute their stories without paying for a plan.

## **Can I start free and upgrade later?**

Yes. You can create a Starter Room and upgrade that same Room when you need more contributions, storage, time or features. Contributions already received remain in the Room.

## **What counts as one contribution?**

Each story submitted by a guest counts as one contribution. A contribution may contain a written story, photo, video or voice note.

## **What happens after 30 days on the Starter Room?**

New contributions close. You can still access and download the stories already collected or upgrade the Room to continue.

## **What happens after 12 months on a Full Room?**

Ulo will remind the organiser before online access ends. The organiser can download the complete Room or move it into an active Family Archive. A Full Room does not renew automatically.

**Frequently asked questions continued**

## **What is the difference between a Full Room and a Family Archive?**

A Full Room is designed for one occasion, such as a wedding, birthday, burial or memorial. A Family Archive is an ongoing private space where family members preserve stories across different people, generations and moments.

## **Does a Family Archive include Full Event or Tribute Rooms?**

Not at launch. Full Event and Tribute Rooms are purchased separately. A completed Room can, however, be moved into an active Family Archive.

## **Can I cancel the Family Archive?**

Yes. You can cancel at any time. Your subscription remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

## **Will the Family Archive renew automatically?**

Yes. Monthly and yearly Family Archive subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the next billing date.

## **Can I download my stories?**

Yes. Starter Rooms allow individual downloads. Full Rooms and Family Archives include bulk download.

## **Who controls access?**

The organiser or family administrator controls invitations and access. Ulo spaces are private by default unless the organiser chooses to share them.

# 7. Developer implementation notes {#developer-implementation-notes}

**LAUNCH RULES**

**Keep the pricing page simple, local and explicit.**

**Three cards only** Show Starter Room, Full Ulo Room and Family Archive. Do not create separate prices for Event, Celebration Tribute and Memorial Tribute Rooms.

**Regional display** Auto-select a likely region where reliable, but always allow the customer to change it manually before checkout.

**One displayed currency** Show only the selected regional price on each card. The regional matrix in this document is an internal implementation reference.

**Exact Full Room label** Place \'One-off payment per Room\' immediately beneath the Full Room price.

**No equivalent wording** For Rest of Africa, display \'US\$19\'. Do not display \'US\$19 equivalent\'.

**Guests remain free** Repeat \'Guests contribute free\' beneath the Full Room call-to-action.

**Clear renewal behaviour** State that Full Rooms do not auto-renew and Family Archive subscriptions do.

**Checkout truth** The checkout total, tax treatment, renewal terms and selected currency must match the pricing-page copy.

**Retention reminders** Implement reminders before a Starter Room closes and before a Full Room\'s online access ends.

**Policy access** Place Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy links near the pricing footer and checkout.

## Pre-publication acceptance check

> **Product owner:** Confirm contribution limits, storage, file formats, guest access, moderation, downloads, slideshow creation, Archive transfer, collection periods and online access periods.
>
> **Finance and legal:** Confirm currencies, tax treatment, automatic renewal disclosures, cancellation behaviour, refunds and all policy links.
>
> **Final release rule:** If any product behaviour differs from this document, update the customer-facing wording before launch. Do not publish a feature or promise that cannot be demonstrated in the live product.
