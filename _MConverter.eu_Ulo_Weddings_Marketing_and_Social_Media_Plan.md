# **Executive Summary**

Ulo should launch publicly with a singular wedding use case rather than trying to explain the full event platform at once. The recommended launch product is Ulo Wedding Room: a private room where wedding guests can contribute the photographs, videos and messages they capture, so the couple can keep the wider story of the wedding together.

The product remains part of the wider Ulo master brand. Weddings become the first acquisition wedge, with Family Archive, Tributes and broader Events introduced after the wedding proposition has achieved market recognition and repeatable sales.

**LAUNCH GOALS**

- Make Ulo immediately understandable through one highly relatable problem: wedding memories scattered across hundreds of guest phones and chats.

- Sell the Ulo Wedding Room at ₦15,000 one-off per wedding, with guests contributing free of charge.

- Use wedding vendors as the main distribution engine, supported by Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and selective paid media.

- Validate the model with 100 paid Wedding Rooms in the first launch month.

- Turn wedding customers into future Family Archive and other Ulo customers.

**IMPORTANT PLANNING NOTE**

*The commercial targets in this document are management targets, not forecasts. Ulo does not yet have enough live conversion data to confirm customer acquisition cost, conversion rate, partner productivity or contribution margin. These should be measured during the launch and used to refine pricing and spend.*

2\. Fast shots of guests recording, laughing, dancing, taking selfies, aunties filming and bridesmaids capturing spontaneous moments.

3\. Question: "Where do all those memories go?"

4\. Show the fragmentation: WhatsApp, Instagram, phones and random message threads.

5\. Resolve: contributions begin appearing in one Ulo Wedding Room.

6\. Close: "Bring your wedding memories home."

**End card: Ulo Wedding Room \| ₦15,000 one-off \| One Wedding. One Room.**

## **Customer journey**

| **Step** | **Moment** | **What happens** |
|----|----|----|
| 1 | Couple buys | Create a Ulo Wedding Room for ₦15,000. |
| 2 | Room created | The couple receives a wedding link/QR for their room. |

# **3. Distribution Strategy: Ulo Wedding Partners** {#distribution-strategy-ulo-wedding-partners}

The launch should not depend on selling one bride or groom at a time. The strongest scale opportunity is to make Ulo a product that Nigerian wedding vendors routinely recommend or bundle into their own packages.

## **Priority partner categories**

- Wedding planners

- Photographers

- Videographers

- Wedding content creators

- MCs

- Invitation designers

- Event decorators

- Bridal businesses

- Event venues

- Wedding media/platforms

## **Recommended partner economics**

| **Item** | **Amount** | **Meaning** |
|----|----|----|
| Public room price | ₦15,000 | Paid once per wedding |

## **Bundle strategy**

A photographer or planner can include the Ulo Wedding Room inside a larger package. This reduces the number of separate buying decisions for the couple and can produce higher-volume distribution than direct advertising alone.

**Illustrative volume scenario: 100 active vendors x 4 rooms per month = 400 rooms per month. At ₦15,000 each, that is ₦6,000,000 gross sales. At a ₦3,000 partner commission, ₦4,800,000 remains before other costs.**

1\. Phase 1: Lagos - concentrate launch content, vendor recruitment and paid testing.

2\. Phase 2: Abuja and Port Harcourt - expand once messaging and partner onboarding work.

3\. Phase 3: Other Nigerian cities and Nigerian diaspora markets.

*This is an operating recommendation, not a claim that these are definitively the three largest wedding markets.*

# **5. Social Media Channel Strategy** {#social-media-channel-strategy}

| **Channel** | **Primary role** | **Recommended cadence** | **What to publish** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Instagram | Showroom + trust | 6 feed posts/week + Stories daily | Reels, carousels, product demos, couple/vendor collaborations, testimonials |
| TikTok | Discovery | 5-7 native videos/week | POVs, humour, emotional wedding moments, guest-perspective stories, fast product reveals |
| WhatsApp | Conversion + forwarding | Always-on | 15s/30s demos, pricing cards, partner cards, testimonials, direct room links |
| Facebook | Retargeting + family reach | Cross-post best content | Best Reels, family-facing posts, Meta ads, wedding/community groups |
| LinkedIn | Founder narrative + partnerships | Approx. 1 wedding-related post/week | Storytelling philosophy, product lessons, partnership/business updates |

## **Channel principles**

- Instagram should make Ulo feel desirable and credible, not like generic software.

- TikTok content should be native, fast and culturally recognisable rather than polished Instagram adverts reposted unchanged.

- WhatsApp assets must be designed to be forwarded. The ideal conversion sentence is: "My photographer/planner sent me Ulo."

- LinkedIn is for the founder story and partnerships, not the main bride-and-groom sales channel.

## **Visual direction**

- Avoid generic SaaS visuals and repeated floating-phone mock-ups.

- Avoid obvious AI-looking Nigerian wedding imagery or fake testimonial aesthetics.

- Use real Nigerian weddings across different scales, traditions and styles where rights/consent permit.

- Balance luxury aspiration with accessibility: Ulo should feel appropriate at both modest and premium weddings.

- Keep the product interface simple and secondary to the human wedding moment.

# **9. Paid Marketing Test Plan** {#paid-marketing-test-plan}

Paid media should begin as structured testing, not large-scale spend. The purpose is to learn which promise gets the lowest-cost qualified purchase.

| **Test creative** | **Core message** |
|----|----|
| A - Lost photos | Where did all the guest photos go? |
| B - Guests\' perspective | Your photographer sees one story; your guests see many more. |
| C - QR demo | Show exactly how a guest contributes in seconds. |
| D - Family emotion | Lead with an irreplaceable parent/grandparent/friend moment. |
| E - Price/value | ₦15,000 one-off for one wedding room. |

*Initial management guardrail: test towards a customer acquisition cost of roughly ₦3,000-₦4,500 (20-30% of the ₦15,000 selling price). This is a proposed threshold, not a confirmed market CAC. If paid acquisition cannot approach acceptable economics, shift spend and attention towards vendor/referral acquisition.*
